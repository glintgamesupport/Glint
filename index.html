Common questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?
  1. Open Glint and tap the gear button in the bottom corner.
  2. Tap Restore purchases.
  3. Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with, if asked.

"Remove ads" comes back on any device signed in to your Apple ID. Hint packs are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads.

Tap the gear button, then Restore purchases. Forced ads stop right away. Optional videos for free hints stay available, and those only play when you choose.

How do hints work?

Tap Hint and the right mirror pulses. Tapping it always moves you closer to the solution. You start with free hints, get more for finishing each world and each daily puzzle, and can watch a short video or buy a pack when you run out.

What do the stars mean?

Each level has a par: the fewest taps that solve it. Match par for three stars, finish within two taps of par for two, and any solve earns one. You can replay any level for a better score.

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight on your phone's clock. Everyone gets the same puzzle that day. Mondays are gentle and Sundays are the toughest. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress, stars and hints are saved only on your phone, and deleting Glint erases them. Your Remove Ads purchase can always be restored.

Sound or vibration stopped working.

Check the switches under the gear button, and make sure your iPhone isn't in Silent mode. After unlocking your phone, sound comes back on your first tap. If it still doesn't, close Glint completely and open it again.

Can I turn off the music but keep sound effects?

Yes. Tap the gear button and switch off Music. Sound effects and vibration have their own switches.
