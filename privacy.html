Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version: Glint has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Apple through RevenueCat. Those services receive the limited information described below.

Information stored on your phone

Glint saves your level progress, stars, hint count, daily puzzle streak and settings (sound, music, vibration) on your device. This information stays on your phone and isn't sent to us. Deleting the app erases it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glint shows ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device and app information, such as device model, iOS version and app version;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it, such as in the EU and UK, Glint asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Learn more in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Glint never sees your payment details. RevenueCat helps Glint confirm your purchases and restore "Remove ads". It receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, not your name or Apple ID email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't run our own servers that store information about you.

Children

Glint is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier, or limit ad personalization, in iPhone settings.
  • Delete all locally stored game data by tapping Reset progress in the game's settings, or by deleting the app.
  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop interstitial ads.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
